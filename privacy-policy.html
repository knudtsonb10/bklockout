Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

BK Lockout and Key ("we," "us," or "our") operates as a mobile locksmith business serving Mesa, Gilbert, and Chandler, Arizona. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you contact us or use our services.

Information We Collect

When you contact us for locksmith services, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Location or address where service is needed
  • Vehicle information (for automotive lockout services)
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide when requesting service

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide the locksmith services you requested
  • Communicate with you about your service request
  • Send a Google review link following completed service
  • Request your pinned location to navigate to you efficiently
  • Respond to questions or follow-up inquiries

SMS and Text Messaging

If you contact us or consent to receive text messages from BK Lockout and Key, we may send you SMS messages related to your service request, including service confirmations, arrival updates, location requests, and review links. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties. Your information is used exclusively to fulfill your service request and communicate with you directly.

Data Retention

We retain basic contact and service information only as long as necessary to fulfill the service and comply with any applicable legal obligations. We do not maintain permanent customer databases for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
